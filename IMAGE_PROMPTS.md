# Rang Décor: ChatGPT image prompts

## How the visualizer images work (read first)
The old visualizer is a room PNG with a **transparent wall/surface area**. The chosen swatch is tiled behind it, so the pattern shows through the cutout.
ChatGPT cannot reliably output a true transparent area. So every prompt below asks for the surface in **flat pure magenta #FF00FF**. I remove the magenta with a script and make that area transparent (and keep the shadows).

Rules for every room prompt (already inside the prompts):
- One flat, evenly coloured magenta surface with straight, crisp edges. No texture, gradient, or reflection on it.
- Nothing magenta anywhere else in the room.
- Furniture or plants may overlap the magenta area. That is fine, and it gives depth.
- Landscape 3:2, 1536x1024, photorealistic, soft daylight, no text, no logos, no people.
- Generate each room 2 or 3 times and pick the cleanest magenta edges.
